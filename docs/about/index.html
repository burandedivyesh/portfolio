A Little About Me

I'm Divyesh, someone who enjoys figuring things out, experimenting with ideas, and occasionally getting lost in a project simply because I want to understand how it works.

How I Got Here

My journey into software development wasn't exactly a straight line.

I started with an undergraduate degree in Instrumentation and Control Engineering. While my studies introduced me to engineering and technical problem-solving, I gradually found myself more interested in programming and the possibilities that came with it.

That curiosity eventually led me to pursue a Master's in Data Science at Heriot-Watt University.

Studying Data Science allowed me to explore programming, machine learning, statistics, and computational experimentation. More importantly, it reinforced something I'd already started to realise: I enjoy the process of investigating problems, trying different approaches, and understanding why something works.

I don't see my engineering background and my interest in software as separate things. They're different parts of the same journey, shaped by a desire to understand and build.

Learning by Building

I've always found that I understand something better when I can experiment with it myself.

Reading about a concept is one thing. Implementing it, making mistakes, debugging unexpected behaviour, and finally seeing it work is something entirely different.

That's one of the reasons I enjoy building things from scratch. Even when a ready-made tool exists, there's something valuable about understanding what's happening underneath.

Not every experiment turns into a finished project, and not every idea works out the way I expect. But I usually come away having learned something.

Beyond the Code

When I'm not working on a project or exploring something new, there's a good chance I'm following Formula 1.

I'm a Max Verstappen and Red Bull fan. What fascinates me about F1 is how engineering, strategy, driver skill, and split-second decisions come together. There's something exciting about a sport where months of preparation can come down to a few moments on the track.

Beyond F1, I enjoy gaming, reading, and travelling. I also have a particular appreciation for background scores from films and games. I love how music can create an atmosphere or tell a story without needing a single word.

These interests give me something to look forward to outside programming, and occasionally, another rabbit hole to disappear into.

What's Next?

I don't have a perfectly mapped-out list of everything I want to learn or build.

There are plenty of technologies I'd like to explore, ideas I'd like to try, and problems I'd like to understand better.

For now, I'm focused on continuing to learn, creating things that interest me, and documenting that journey as honestly as I can.

This website is part of that process.
